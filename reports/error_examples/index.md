# Error gallery — model 640 — val split

Assignment §10.6. Generated by `src/evaluate/error_gallery.py`.

- operating threshold: conf 0.21, matcher IoU 0.5 (chosen on validation in §10.4)
- ground truth: 500 images, 3854 annotations (3806 non-crowd + 48 iscrowd kept as ignore regions)
- left panel = ground truth (green), right panel = predictions above the threshold (red); the error itself is gold, thickened and arrowed
- `taxonomy.csv` counts every error; its `likely_cause`, `layer` and `next_experiment` columns are **left empty for the human** to fill in after looking at these panels
- one real-world mistake can produce two rows: a wrong-class box is both a spurious detection (`class_confusion`) and an object nobody found (a `missed_*` row)

## Dense-scene statistic (scene-level, not a per-object category)

Mean per-image recall in dense images (more than 20 non-crowd ground-truth instances in the image) is **0.2272** over 33 images, against **0.4045** over 462 sparse images.
